# Assets and licenses

The original 120 BPM score is generated deterministically by scripts/audio.mjs.
Its source is part of this project. No third-party music recording is used.

Cinematic SFX are from Mixkit under its Sound Effects Free License:
https://mixkit.co/license/#sfxFree. The source names, exact URLs, hashes,
durations, and measured peak positions are in src/audio-manifest.json.
Downloaded raw audio is ignored; run `npm run audio` to prepare it locally.
Only the finished audiovisual work is distributed here.

Fonts are Cormorant Garamond, Source Sans 3, and IBM Plex Mono from the
Google Fonts repository under their SIL Open Font Licenses. The preparation
script downloads each font together with its OFL.txt. Font binaries are ignored.

Product imagery and screenshots are captured from this project's actual
frontend and verified agent output. src/evidence.json records the source checks and
image hashes. The portrait was generated by the agent during the live gate.

Motion references: video-shotcraft by Vincentwei1021, spotlight-hero-card and
row-embed; hyperframes-motion-reel-skill by Sunwood-ai-labs for the beat grid and
kinetic showreel direction. PageCam layout-zoom math is adapted from shotcraft.

Remotion is free for individuals and companies with three or fewer employees.
This is a personal hackathon project. See https://www.remotion.dev/license for
the current terms. HyperFrames is Apache-2.0; its engine is not used here.
